# Coffee Sales Analysis: Product Preferences & Payment Behavior

# Project Overview

Performed exploratory data analysis on coffee sales transaction data to identify popular coffee products, preferred payment methods, and revenue trends.

# Tools Used

Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn

# Analysis

Cleaned and processed transaction data, then analyzed product demand, payment preferences, and sales performance using data visualizations.

# Key Insights

Latte and Espresso are the top-selling coffee products.

Credit cards and mobile payments are the most preferred payment methods.

A small group of coffee products generates the majority of total sales revenue.

# Business Recommendations

Focus inventory and promotions on high-demand products and encourage digital payment options to improve customer experience.
