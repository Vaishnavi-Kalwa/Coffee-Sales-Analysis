
# Coffee Sales Analysis: Product Preferences & Payment Behavior

# Project Impact

Conducted an exploratory data analysis (EDA) on coffee sales transaction data to identify trending coffee products, preferred payment methods, and top revenue-generating items. The analysis delivers actionable insights to help businesses optimize product offerings, inventory planning, and customer engagement strategies.

# Business Objective

The primary goal of this project was to support data-driven decision-making by:

Identifying the most popular coffee types

Determining the most preferred payment methods

Analyzing which coffee products generate the highest sales revenue

Understanding customer purchasing behavior to improve marketing and operational strategies

# Dataset Overview

The dataset contains transaction-level coffee sales data, including:

Coffee Type (Latte, Espresso, Cappuccino, etc.)

Payment Method (Cash, Credit Card, Mobile Payment)

Quantity Sold

Total Sales

Time of Purchase (where available)

# Tools & Technologies

Python – Data analysis and visualization

Pandas – Data cleaning, preprocessing, and aggregation

Matplotlib & Seaborn – Visual analysis and trend exploration

# Data Preparation & Analysis

Cleaned raw transaction data by:

Handling missing values

Standardizing categorical variables

Formatting date and time fields

Performed exploratory data analysis (EDA) to examine:

Coffee sales trends by product category

Payment method preferences

Sales volume and revenue distribution

Used visualizations including:

Bar charts

Pie charts

Histograms

Time series plots

# Key Insights
Product Preferences

Latte and Espresso consistently emerged as the top-selling coffee types, indicating strong and stable customer demand.

Other coffee categories showed lower but consistent sales, highlighting opportunities for targeted promotions.

Payment Method Trends

Credit card payments were the most commonly used method, followed by mobile payments.

Cash usage was comparatively lower, suggesting a growing preference for digital and convenient payment options.

Sales Performance

High-performing coffee types contributed the majority of total sales revenue.

Sales trends over time revealed consistent demand patterns for popular products, supporting inventory optimization.

Purchasing Behavior

Explored correlations between coffee type and payment method, providing insights into customer purchasing behavior and preferences.

# Business Recommendations

Prioritize inventory and promotions for high-demand products like Latte and Espresso.

Encourage digital payment options to enhance customer convenience and checkout efficiency.

Use sales trends to support demand forecasting and inventory planning.

Design targeted marketing campaigns for lower-performing coffee categories to boost visibility and sales.

Translated raw sales data into clear, actionable insights

Demonstrated strong EDA, visualization, and analytical reasoning skills

Supported business decisions related to product strategy and customer experience
