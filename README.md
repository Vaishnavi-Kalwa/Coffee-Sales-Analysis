
# Coffee Sales Analysis – Identifying Trends in Product Preference and Payment Methods
# Objective:
To analyze coffee sales data in order to identify the most trending coffee types, the most preferred payment methods, and determine which coffee products generate the highest sales. The goal is to provide actionable insights to optimize product offerings and improve customer engagement strategies.

# Project Description:
The dataset, containing transaction-level sales data, was cleaned and analyzed using Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn). Performed data preprocessing including handling missing values, standardizing categorical values, and formatting date-related fields. Conducted exploratory data analysis (EDA) to understand customer preferences by visualizing trends across different coffee types, payment methods, and sales volume using bar plots, pie charts, histograms, and time series plots.

Key variables analyzed included:

Coffee Type (e.g., Latte, Espresso, Cappuccino)

Payment Method (e.g., Cash, Credit Card, Mobile Payment)

Quantity and Total Sales

Time of Purchase (if available)

Correlations between coffee type and payment method were also explored to understand purchasing behavior more deeply. Sales trends were segmented by coffee category and visualized to reveal top-selling products over time.

# Conclusion:
The analysis revealed that certain coffee types, such as Latte and Espresso, consistently led in sales, indicating strong customer preference. Credit card was the most commonly used payment method, followed by mobile payments. These insights suggest that product inventory and marketing efforts should focus on high-performing coffee types and that businesses may benefit from emphasizing convenience in payment options. The findings provide a clear direction for enhancing product strategy and improving the customer purchase experience.
